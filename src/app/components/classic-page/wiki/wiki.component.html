Overview

You are the last human alive in a ruined city.

Each night, waves of zombiesattack your defenses.
Each day, you dig, build, and learn in an effort to survive one more night.
Are you ready to climb the ranks and dominate the leaderboard?

Defense and Attacks

All buildings (except special ones) add defense points.
The zombie wave increases nightly.
≥ → You survive.
< → You die.

Buildings

Buildings cost resources and time to construct. The buildings increase the defense of your city.

Building Description Detail
Blueprint Workshop Increases discovery odds for new building plans (probability is 0.7 per level). You will have a choice for your discoveries
Archives Increases discovery odds for new skill levels (probability is 0.5 per level). No choice

A building's defense value is subject to a degree of random variability, which can fluctuate by up to ±20% from one game to the next.

These variations will be displayed by a small percentage indicator next to the building's defense rating. 52 +19% or 35 -7%

Deck and collection

Your collection holds every building plan you own. Before each game, your deck of 20 plans (5 epic at most) is the only pile your starting plans and the Blueprint Workshop discoveries are drawn from.

Get new plans by opening boosters in the shop. Duplicates are kept for later.
Digs find every resource with the same probability: a deck that needs a bit of every resource builds faster.
Once 15 buildings of your deck reach their max level, every completed building goes back into the draw pile. Picking one again unlocks a new set of levels for it.

Skills

Each skill improves a specific aspect of survival. Effects are linear per level.

Skill Effect per Level Level max Effect
Insomniac Wake up 15 min earlier each day. 2 hours earlier wake-up.
Digger Reduce search time linearly. Action time divided by 2.
Builder Reduce building time linearly. Action time divided by 2.
Fast Learner Reduce learning time linearly. Action time divided by 2.

Ranked Mode

Don't hesitate to play in ranked to get more (+50%)!

A ranked game is a duel: two players face the exact same hordes, one after the other, and the best survivor wins ranked points.

Finding an opponent

When you start a ranked game, you join the oldest game whose first player has already finished. You face their hordes, but you won't see their score until you fall too.
If no game is waiting, a new one is created and you play first: your result waits for the next ranked player to take up the challenge.

The same hordes for both players

Both players share the same random seeds: same zombie waves night after night, same starting skills, same resources found dig after dig. Each player brings their own deck: building it well is part of the duel.
Your permanent bonuses still apply in ranked.

Who wins?

Order Criterion
1 The player who survives the most days wins.
2 Same day: the highest defense on the night of the fall wins.
3 Same day and same defense: it's a draw, nobody wins or loses points.

Ranked points

Every player starts with 500 points. Points are computed like chess Elo: what the winner earns, the loser loses (at most 48 per game). Beating a stronger player earns more, losing to them costs less.
Your opponent has… If you win If you lose
the same points as you +24 -24
200 points more +36 -12
400 points more +44 -4
Points are given once both players have fallen. Until then, the game shows as pending in your history, then as Victory, Defeat or Draw.

Ranked money

At the fall of your city, you earn 50% more money (half of the zombies repelled), win or lose. It is credited right away, without waiting for your opponent.

Inactivity

If you are the second player and your city stays inactive for 48 hours while your opponent has finished for more than 48 hours, you lose by forfeit, with your city's current day and defense. Your city is not lost: it goes on as a normal game, without ranked money.
If you play first and don't finish within 30 days, the ranked game is cancelled: your city goes on and counts as a normal game, without ranked points.
Climb the ranked leaderboard!

Bonuses

Use the money you accumulate across all your games in the shop to purchase powerful permanent bonuses . Any bonus bought will enhance your abilities in all future games.

These bonuses will be automatically applied to all your future games, giving you a strategic advantage.

Sleeping

Can't fit another action before midnight?
Sleep early to carry time over to the next day. By pressing this button: Sleep
Note: only half the time is recovered, up to 3h (e.g., sleep at 10pm = 2h saved = 1h bonus tomorrow; sleep at 6pm or earlier = 3h bonus).
