Overview

You are the last human alive in a ruined city.

Each night, waves of zombies attack your defenses.

Each day, you scavenge, build, and learn in an effort to survive one more night.

Are you ready to climb the ranks and dominate the leaderboard?

Buildings

Buildings cost resources and time to construct. The buildings increase the defense of your city.

Building Max Level Description Detail
Architect Shelter 3 Increases discovery odds for new building plans (probability is 0.7 per level). You will have a choice for your discoveries
Library 3 Increases discovery odds for new skill levels (probability is 0.5 per level). No choice

A building's defense value is subject to a degree of random variability, which can fluctuate by up to ±20% from one game to the next.

These variations will be displayed by a small percentage indicator next to the building's defense rating.

Skills

Each skill improves a specific aspect of survival. Effects are linear per level.

Skill Max Level Effect per Level Level max Effect
Insomniac 8 Wake up 15 min earlier each day. 2 hours earlier wake-up.
Digger 5 Reduce search time linearly. Action time divided by 2.
Builder 5 Reduce building time linearly. Action time divided by 2.
Fast Learner 5 Reduce learning time linearly. Action time divided by 2.

⚔️ Defense and Attacks

  • All buildings (except special ones) add defense points.
  • The zombie horde increases nightly.
  • Defense ≥ Zombies → Survive; otherwise you die.

💡 Experience & Levels

Gain XP/Money equal to zombies defeated.

Player level uses a logarithmic curve. You can buy permanent bonuses with your accumulated money for future games

Ranked Mode

Asynchronous deterministic competition using seeded runs. You will not be able to blame luck!

Compared performance: days survived, defenses built.

Elo-like ranked point system.

Fight for the leaderboard.

💰 Bonuses

Use the money you accumulate across all your games to purchase powerful permanent bonuses.

These improvements will be automatically applied to all your future games, giving you a strategic advantage.

For example, you can purchase upgrades that significantly increase the base defense provided by every building you construct.

🧩 Strategy Tips

Upgrade Libraries to unlock higher skill caps.

Architect Shelters unlock stronger defense plans.
